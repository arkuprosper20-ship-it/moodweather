Your privacy, in plain words

  • No names

    We never ask who you are. There's nothing to type and nothing that links back to you.

  • No accounts

    No sign-up, no login, no profile. You can't be tracked between check-ins.

  • Group patterns only

    Your check-in is combined with everyone else's, so the school sees trends — like “Cloudy was common this week” — never individual people.

  • Small groups stay hidden

    If fewer than 5 students share a group, its results are hidden so no one can be picked out.

  • No diagnoses

    MoodWeather never labels anyone and never gives medical advice.

Your once-a-day limit is remembered only on this device, in your browser.

How it works

Four steps, and your mood never travels with your name.

1. You tap a mood

One weather icon on your phone. No name, no account, no email.

2. A counter goes up by one

Your tap adds exactly 1 to a group total. Nothing else is stored about you.

3. Summaries are protected

Only group totals are saved. Groups under 5 students are hidden automatically.

4. Staff see the weather map

Teachers and counselors see patterns for the whole grade — never you.
